## 💡 About Unboxx Business

**Unboxx Business** is a platform built to empower beginners by providing everything they need to **kickstart an online business**—from coding templates to learning tutorials, free resources, toolkits, and much more.

Whether you’re building a website, launching a digital product, or starting your freelance journey, **we help you unbox your potential.**

---

## 📦 What We Offer

- ✅ Pre-built **code templates** (HTML, CSS, React, JS)
- ✅ Beginner-friendly **tutorials** & walkthroughs
- ✅ Ready-to-use **UI kits** and landing pages
- ✅ Exclusive **free resources** for entrepreneurs
- ✅ Project ideas for hands-on learning
- ✅ Mobile apps for **on-the-go learning**
- ✅ Design assets (Figma, Adobe XD)
- ✅ Real-time project collaboration
