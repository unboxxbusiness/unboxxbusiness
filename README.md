# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is AI Search Optimization?

AI Search Optimization is the process of improving an organization's digital information so AI-powered search and answer systems can more easily discover, understand, retrieve, summarize, and potentially cite that information.

## What is GEO?

GEO stands for **Generative Engine Optimization**. It focuses on improving visibility within generative AI search experiences and answer engines.

## What is AEO?

AEO stands for **Answer Engine Optimization**. It focuses on creating structured, direct, useful answers for systems that respond to user questions rather than simply returning a list of links.

## Does AI Search Optimization replace SEO?

No.

Technical SEO, content quality, crawlability, authority, and search fundamentals remain important. AI Search Optimization extends traditional search optimization into the emerging world of AI answer engines and generative search.

## Can AI Search Optimization help ChatGPT visibility?

It can help make an organization's public information clearer, more structured, authoritative, and accessible to systems that retrieve web information. No legitimate agency can guarantee that ChatGPT or another AI system will always mention or cite a particular company.

## Can AI Search Optimization help Perplexity visibility?

The same principle applies. Strong technical foundations, authoritative information, useful content, clear entities, and accessible evidence can improve the quality of a site's information for retrieval-based answer experiences. Visibility and citations cannot be guaranteed.

## What businesses benefit from AI Search Optimization?

Organizations that depend on digital discovery can benefit, particularly universities, healthcare providers, real estate organizations, SaaS companies, professional services, franchises, and multi-location businesses.

## What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO primarily focuses on visibility in search engines and search result pages.

GEO focuses on visibility within generative AI systems and AI-generated answers.

The strongest strategy combines both.
